## Level
Graduate (Maestría en Economía)

## Course Overview

These materials develop the mathematical tools used to formulate and solve dynamic macroeconomic models. Topics include ordinary differential equations, linear and nonlinear dynamic systems, phase diagrams, difference equations, optimal control, and dynamic programming.

The files remain in their original classroom language.
