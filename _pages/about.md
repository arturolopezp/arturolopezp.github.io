I am an economist interested in monetary economics, small open economy macroeconomics, and empirical macroeconomics.

This website brings together my research materials, replication files, teaching resources, and independent computational projects. The first course collection available here is [Macroeconomics II](/teaching/), taught at CIDE in Fall 2026.

Additional research and project repositories will be added as they are reorganized and documented.
