One-Period General Equilibrium Lab

Explore shocks, market curves, equilibrium allocations, and the equations behind a one-period economy.

Explore the one-period closed-economy model from Macroeconomics II. Change preferences, technology, and government spending; compare equilibria; then test your predictions before revealing a shock.

Model setup: Household preferences over consumption \(C\) and leisure \(\ell\) are represented by the utility function \(U(C,\ell)\). The household allocates its unit time endowment between labor \(N\) and leisure, so \(N+\ell=1\). A representative firm produces output according to \(Y=z f(N)\), where \(z\) denotes productivity. The government finances public spending through lump-sum taxes, \(T=G\). The consumption good is the numeraire.
