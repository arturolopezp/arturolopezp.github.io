Microeconomics I

Graduate microeconomic theory problem sets, quizzes, solutions, and supplementary notes.

Instructor: Arturo López (Teaching Assistant)

Term: Fall

Location: Centro de Investigación y Docencia Económicas (CIDE)

Level

Graduate (Maestría en Economía)

Course Overview

These materials cover the mathematical foundations of consumer theory, choice under uncertainty, production, and market structure.

The files remain in their original classroom language.
